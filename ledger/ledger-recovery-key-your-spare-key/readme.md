# Ledger Recovery Key: your spare key

**Why this matters:** A lost or broken device should be a small problem, not a disaster.

The **Ledger Recovery Key** is a physical NFC smart card; Ledger calls it "your private spare key." It stores an encrypted backup of your wallet access, and inside the card sits its own Secure Element, also certified EAL6, so your backup gets the same class of chip that guards your signer. The card is PIN-protected and rated IP68 against dust and water.

**Backing up takes three steps:** hold the card to the back of your device, create a unique PIN for the card, tap to transfer. The encrypted data moves from device to card completely offline. **Restoring is even shorter:** tap the card on a new Ledger device, enter your PIN, and your access is back.

Three facts to keep straight:

- **It is optional.** The card ships in the box with the touchscreen signers and is also sold separately. You decide whether to use it.
- **It needs NFC.** It works with the Nano Gen5, Flex, and Stax. The button classics have no NFC, so they cannot use it.
- **Ledger Recovery Key is not Ledger Recover.** Ledger Recover is a separate, paid subscription recovery service. The names look similar; the products are different.

And one more honest note: your seed phrase on paper still works as the classic backup, exactly as Module 2 described, and the card does not replace it. Treat the card as a convenience layer: fast to restore from, protected by a PIN and a Secure Element, but a supplement rather than a requirement.

> **Key idea:** The Recovery Key card is a PIN-protected, fully offline spare key, and it is not the Ledger Recover subscription.
