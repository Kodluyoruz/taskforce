# The three touchscreen signers

**Why this matters:** You cannot move your signing boundary onto hardware until you choose the hardware.

In Module 2 you met the chip; in Module 3, the app. Now you choose the device that connects them. After the incident file that opens this module, the purchase rule should need no argument: **buy from ledger.com or an authorized reseller, never second-hand.**

Ledger's current touchscreen line has three signers: the **Ledger Nano Gen5**, the **Ledger Flex**, and the **Ledger Stax**. All three share the same base package:

- an **E Ink touchscreen** (the low-power screen technology from e-book readers)
- three connections: USB-C, Bluetooth, and **NFC** (near-field communication, data over a short tap)
- a Secure Element certified at **CC EAL6**
- a **Ledger Recovery Key** card in the box (see the Recovery Key lesson)

Here is how they differ:

| | Ledger Nano Gen5 | Ledger Flex | Ledger Stax |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | ~$179 | ~$249 | ~$399 |
| **Screen** | 2.8" E Ink touchscreen, monochrome | 2.8" E Ink touchscreen, 16 grayscale levels | 3.7" curved E Ink touchscreen, 16 grayscale levels |
| **Standout extras** | Newest of the three (launched October 2025); lowest touchscreen price | Gorilla Glass protection; sharper grayscale display | Qi wireless charging; Magnet Shell cover included |

"16 grayscale levels" means the screen renders 16 shades of gray, so text and transaction details look smoother than on a monochrome panel.

**One honest note about the older devices.** Ledger still sells two button-based classics, the **Nano S Plus** (~$49) and the **Nano X** (~$99). They cost less and they still keep private keys offline, which is most of the battle. But Clear Signing and Transaction Check, the verification features at the heart of Module 5, are tied to the touchscreen line, and this course teaches you to verify everything you sign. That is why it focuses on the three touchscreen signers.

> **Key idea:** The Nano Gen5, Flex, and Stax share one hardware base; they differ in screen, extras, and price.
