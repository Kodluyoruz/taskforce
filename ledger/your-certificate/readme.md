# Your certificate

Complete all modules, and you earn a digital certificate, issued by Rise In through its platform. **[Confirm with team: where the certificate appears and how it is delivered]**

**What does it actually mean?** It records practical knowledge rather than attendance: you can explain the signing boundary, verify an address on a device screen, and read a transaction before you approve it. Plenty of developers work in crypto for years without building those habits, and after the incidents in this course you know what skipping them costs. Your certificate shows you built them on purpose.

It is digital, so share it where it helps: LinkedIn, your CV or portfolio, your team.

One honest note, in the spirit of the whole course: a certificate proves you passed a course, and your daily habits are what protect your crypto — checking the device screen, rejecting blind approvals, keeping your seed phrase offline. **The certificate is the record; the habits are the security.**
