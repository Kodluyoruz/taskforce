# Watch Mode: explore before you buy

Many learners wait: "I'll explore Ledger Wallet when my device arrives." You don't have to wait, and honestly, the order works better reversed: **learn the app first, add the device later.**

**Watch Mode** is Ledger Wallet's device-free mode. Install the app, open it without connecting a signer, and explore everything:

- the full interface, screen by screen
- a portfolio you build and track
- live market data and trending assets
- simulated trades that compare provider rates, the same comparison from the previous lesson

The limit, stated precisely: **Ledger Wallet requires a Ledger signer to authorize transactions.** Without one, no transaction can be signed or broadcast, and the app creates no keys in software. That limit is not a marketing trick; it is the boundary from Module 1, enforced in the product. An app that could sign without hardware would put your keys back into software, which is the exact risk this course started with.

For you, the limit is good news. In Watch Mode there is no seed phrase and there are no keys, so nothing exists yet that you could lose or leak. You can press every button safely.

## Your action step for this module — ten minutes total

1. Download Ledger Wallet free from **ledger.com**, desktop or mobile.
2. Open it and explore in Watch Mode.
3. Build a small portfolio from assets you already follow.
4. Simulate a trade and compare provider rates.
5. Browse Discover and find a dApp you already use.

When your device arrives, the app will already feel familiar, and the device adds the one missing power: approval. Choosing that device is Module 4. And a reminder: finishing this course and getting a Ledger signer earns you the **$10 BTC voucher**; details in Module 8.

> **Key idea:** Install Ledger Wallet today and explore it in Watch Mode. Everything works except signing, because signing belongs to the device.
