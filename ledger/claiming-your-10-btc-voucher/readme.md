# Claiming your $10 BTC voucher

A **voucher** is a code you exchange for something of value, and yours is worth $10 in BTC.

The reward is one milestone with two parts: **finish the course**, then **get a Ledger signer**. Complete both and the voucher is yours to claim. The structure isn't arbitrary: knowledge alone does not protect funds. The course gives you the model, the signer puts the boundary in your hands, and the voucher gives you your first BTC to hold behind it. If you haven't chosen a signer yet, Module 4 compares the options.
