# The signing checklist

This whole module compresses into one routine. Run it every time your device asks for an approval; it takes under a minute. Copy it, screenshot it, keep it where you work.

## The Signing Checklist

1. **Verify the address on the device screen**, not the computer screen.
2. **Read every Clear Signing field:** action, amount, recipient, fees.
3. **Take every Transaction Check warning seriously.** A warning means stop.
4. **Prefer limited approvals**, never unlimited by default.
5. **Review and revoke old allowances** regularly.
6. **Urgency is an attack tool.** Slow down.
7. **Unsure? Reject.** Rejecting costs nothing.

Two items deserve one more line each.

**Urgency (item 6).** Almost every scam adds time pressure, whether it is a mint that closes in minutes or a fake support agent who says your funds are in danger right now. The pressure itself is the attack, engineered to stop you from running items 1 through 5. Legitimate transactions can wait. Feeling rushed is your signal to slow down.

**Rejecting (item 7).** Pressing reject has no cost. Nothing was signed, no funds moved, no fee was lost, and a legitimate transaction can be recreated in two minutes, while a drained account cannot. When anything feels wrong, the default answer is reject.

We'll add one thing from experience: the first couple of weeks of running this checklist feel slow, and around week three it becomes muscle memory that costs you nothing. Every experienced signer we know went through the same curve. Push through the annoying part.
