# Same protection, different fit

**Why this matters:** The signer you enjoy using is the signer you will actually use.

By now the decision is simpler than it looked, because security is equal across the trio. Match the device to your habits and budget instead:

- **Newest tech at the lowest touchscreen price → Nano Gen5 (~$179).** Monochrome screen, every verification feature present.
- **You sign often and want a sharper screen → Flex (~$249).** The 16 grayscale levels render transaction details more smoothly, and Gorilla Glass survives daily carry.
- **The premium desk device → Stax (~$399).** The largest screen of the three, 3.7" and curved, plus Qi wireless charging, so it can sit charged and ready next to your keyboard.

Here is the test I actually recommend. Picture a normal Tuesday: you're about to sign a contract interaction, and the device asks you to read the details. **Which screen would you actually read them on, rather than skim?** Buy that one. A signer that feels comfortable gets checked carefully, a signer that feels annoying gets rushed, and rushing is how the incidents in this course happen. Comfort is a security habit wearing a lifestyle disguise.

Whatever you pick, the important move is the same: all three put your signing boundary onto hardware, which is the step this whole course is about. Your purchase may also count toward the course reward, the **$10 BTC voucher**; Module 8 has the details.
